HAID

House of AI Dining

HAID helps college students pick dining-hall meals that fit their goals, using menus, their schedule and their steps. Health, calendar and location data stay on the iPhone.

HAID is an independent app made by a student. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the University of Michigan, Michigan Dining (MDining), or any other school or company.
