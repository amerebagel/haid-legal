HAID Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026 Contact: doctorsknockers@aol.com

HAID ("House of AI Dining") helps you choose dining-hall meals. We built it to collect as little as possible. Most of what HAID uses never leaves your iPhone.

Independent app

HAID is an independent app made by a student. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or connected to the University of Michigan, Michigan Dining (MDining), or any other school or company. HAID doesn't use any school's logos, official colors, mascots or slogans; its navy-and-gold theme is its own. School and dining hall names appear only to say where the menus come from.

What stays on your iPhone

These are stored only on your device. We never receive them:

  • Health data (steps and active calories from Apple Health or your chosen tracker)
  • Calendar events (used to plan around your classes)
  • Location (used to pick a nearby dining hall)
  • Your goals, food log, favorites, hidden foods, dessert contracts and settings

HAID does not use Health data for advertising or share it with anyone.

Calendar places. To place your calendar events on the map, HAID asks Apple Maps to look up the event's location text (for example "1200 EECS, Ann Arbor"). Event titles, times and other calendar details are never sent anywhere.

What is sent to our server

Dining menus. To show menus, the app requests them from HAID's server. The request contains only your campus and a date range. It contains no name, account, or personal data. If our server doesn't have a day, the app may fetch that day's public menu directly from the dining provider's website.

Food logging. When you describe food you ate and you've allowed it, the text you type is sent through HAID's server to Google Gemini, which reads it and estimates each food's amount and nutrition. HAID asks before the first time and you can turn this off in Settings. HAID uses Gemini's free tier, under which Google may use what it receives to improve its products, so don't include anything personal in a food description. HAID's server passes the text along without storing it. If Gemini is off or unavailable, Apple Intelligence reads the text on your iPhone instead and it never leaves your phone. Either way, nutrition is an estimate you review before saving.

Ratings. If you give a dining hall item a thumbs up or down, HAID's server stores the hall, the item name, your vote and a one-way hash of the random install ID (so you have one vote per item), and shows everyone the totals. Votes aren't linked to you.

  • The install ID is created by the app, isn't linked to your identity, and changes if you reinstall. It's used only to prevent overuse.
  • For rate limiting we keep salted, non-reversible hashes of the install ID and IP address for up to two days.

Feedback and problem reports

If you use Settings → Report a problem, the app writes an email in your own email app addressed to doctorsknockers@aol.com. Nothing is sent until you tap Send. We receive your email address, your message, any screenshots you attach, and, if you leave "Include app details" on, the app version, iOS version, device type, campus, and when menus last loaded. We use this only to fix problems and reply to you. Ask us anytime to delete your messages.

Service providers

  • Supabase hosts HAID's server and database. Like any web service, it receives your IP address with each request and keeps standard logs.
  • Google (Gemini API) receives the food descriptions you type in Log food, if you've allowed it, to read them and estimate nutrition.
  • Apple Maps looks up the location text of calendar events.

We don't sell data, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites.

Children

HAID is intended for college students and isn't directed to children under 13.

Accuracy

Dining hall nutrition comes from the dining provider's posted menus. Food you log by description is estimated by AI (Google Gemini or Apple Intelligence) from typical values. All of it is approximate. HAID isn't medical or dietary advice.

Your choices

  • Deny or revoke Health, Calendar and Location access in iOS Settings.
  • Turn off "Read food with Google Gemini" in Settings to keep descriptions on your iPhone.
  • Delete the app to erase everything stored on your device.
  • Questions or requests: doctorsknockers@aol.com.

Changes

We'll update this page and its date if anything changes.
